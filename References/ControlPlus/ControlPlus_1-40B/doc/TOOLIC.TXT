DAPTOOLS

DAPTOOLS is an on-line database accessible through CompuSERVE and
through the DAP Internet site located at DAP.SVO.COM (192.195.29.5),
which IBM is making available to members of the IBM Personal Software
Products Developer Assistance Programs (DAP).  DAPTOOLS is intended to
facilitate the development of application programs for use with IBM
Personal Software Products operating system platforms including (but
not limited to) OS/2 and PC DOS.  DAPTOOLS includes the following
categories of materials (DAPTOOLS Materials) which you may

CompuSERVE and at the DAP Internet site DAP.SVO.COM (192.195.29.5) in
the DAPTOOLS.300 disk directory.

IBM has not tested, debugged or documented these programs to the same
extent as programs which IBM makes generally available.  IBM cannot
assure that the Development Tools are complete or useable enough to be
